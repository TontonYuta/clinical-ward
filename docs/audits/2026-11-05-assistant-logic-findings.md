# Assistant and medication safety logic audit — 2026-11-05

This review followed the frontend chat dispatch and medication advice paths into backend chat routing, session context, prescription auditing, the DDI detector, notification providers, registry profile formatting, and RAG evidence verification. Findings below are software logic defects demonstrated by source tracing or isolated executions. They do not certify medical content, prescribing policy, or legal compliance.

## Follow-up findings and remaining limits

1. **P1 — Symptom subject mismatch: corrected in the follow-up implementation.** The baseline symptom questions returned CLIOQUINOL/TRIMETHOPRIM adverse-effect monographs. Patient `Trần Thị Mai` additionally matched herbal `Nhân trần` across the phrase `bệnh nhân Trần`, and accented `sốt cao là...` matched herbal `Cao lá` after accent removal. `clinical_subject.py` now classifies symptom cases before drug-candidate searches, clears unrelated prior medication context, and requires matching clinical/guideline subject evidence. `lexical_trie_matcher.py` masks patient references and does not reinterpret explicitly different accented words as drug mentions; genuinely unaccented drug names remain supported. Dedicated regressions preserve explicitly named drug ADR lookups and real herbal-name lookups. Where the corpus lacks matching symptom guidance, the response asks for clinical context instead of inventing a diagnosis or drug recommendation. **The missing general symptom-guidance corpus remains a functional coverage limit.** Globally disabled canned scenarios were not re-enabled to force old medication/protocol assertions to pass.

2. **P1 — Caller-supplied signature attestation: corrected.** Arbitrary `digitalSignature='x'` or `status='signed'` previously produced `doctor_signature_verified=true`. The stateless auditor now returns false because it has no trusted signature-verification operation, while its explanatory text distinguishes reported signature data from a verified signature. Root updated the frontend label to describe verification being unavailable. A server-owned signature verifier remains outside this change; no legal validity claim is made.

3. **P1 — Chat inventory identity/availability: corrected.** Fuzzy ingredient/name joins previously mixed registrations, strengths, units, expired lots and held stock. `chatbot/product_stock.py` now requires exact normalized product registration identity, checks explicit concentration/form consistency, excludes expired/quarantined lots and cold-storage holds using the shared inventory policy, and keeps actual units separate. Unknown product identity cannot infer stock from a name. Operational stock answers are deterministic, cite no clinical monograph as inventory evidence, and distinguish unavailable/unverified stock from a proven zero. Existing physical inventory data still requires correct registration and presentation metadata.

4. **P2 — Chat CPOE authorization: corrected by root integration.** The former deny-one-role policy is now explicit `userRole === 'doctor'` for every matched trigger, including unaccented text. New local-intent regressions cover nurse, pharmacist, director, biomedical engineer and unknown roles. Backend authorization still controls real mutations.

5. **P2 — Empty evidence after DDI cleanup: corrected.** A deterministic fixture reproduced `IndexError` when all-DDI/non-interaction cleanup removed the evidence while an LLM answer was present. The formatter now checks emptiness after cleanup and abstains before indexing a first chunk or exposing the ungrounded generated text.

6. **Notification integration follow-up: corrected by root integration.** Durable per-channel delivery claims avoid repeating a successful email after SMS failure. Unknown outcomes require reconciliation instead of automatic retry. SMTP/GAS/HTML/React outputs retain explicit dose, route, frequency, duration, medication instructions, global clinical notes and slot notes; generated institution/doctor/timing/defaults were removed. Sent/accepted is not delivery confirmation; real provider callbacks, deployment and operational reconciliation remain unverified. See the consolidated audit and manual sink evidence.

7. **P1/P2 — Cold-chain chat status and routing: corrected.** The GSP suggestion and `nhiệt độ kho lạnh` phrases were missing from operational dispatch. The previous handler also invented 4.5°C/MKT values when no row existed and called out-of-range temperatures normal whenever outage/quarantine flags were false. It now recognizes these phrases, requires a recorded and unambiguous cabinet, reports recorded measurements and update time, checks temperature/MKT/VVM/outage/quarantine together, and distinguishes a user's incident description from an actual persisted state change. It does not claim real-time sensor confirmation or vaccine-lot usability from a cabinet snapshot. Missing/ambiguous state and high-temperature regressions pass.

8. **P2 — Unidentified object interpreted as a fuzzy drug: corrected.** `cái này dùng thế nào nhỉ cho tôi biết thêm` previously initiated drug-name searches and generated unrelated candidates. An object with no named medication now returns a bounded abstention asking for its identity, without a drug search, citations or action card.

9. **Joint-monograph coverage retained.** Exact identity priority exposed Primolut N/Normens coverage under the joint source `Norethisterone and norethisterone acetate`. A reviewed explicit identity set accepts the English and source-used Vietnamese/French spellings (`norethisterone`, `norethisterone acetate`, `norethisteron`, `norethisteron acetat`) for this joint INN only. Exact linked/single-INN matches retain priority; longer unrelated substrings and lookalike names remain excluded. The existing Primolut → Normens → Venclexta isolation test passes.

## Baseline failure interpretation

Root's full isolated backend baseline reported **318 passed / 20 failed** before these changes; the root audit report retains all failing names. The symptom-to-Clioquinol/Trimethoprim/Nhân Trần outputs and the offered `Kiểm tra kho lạnh GSP` chip being refused are genuine behavior defects. Empty clinical enrichment data is a data/behavior contract failure requiring separate investigation. Exact expected headings, exact route names, presence of chips explicitly disabled in current code, and an `exact_match` expectation when synthesis honestly reports `fallback_deterministic` are not enough by themselves to establish a safety defect; those assertions need product-contract review. Refusal versus a requested clinical protocol is still a functional gap even when refusing is safer than inventing an answer.

The subsequent targeted **127-case** routing/RAG run produced **122 passed / 5 failed**. Two unchanged baseline assertions demand disabled chips or `exact_match` instead of the honest synthesis status. A cold-chain assertion requires the blanket phrase `Bình thường (An toàn)` and canned incident text; the new behavior reports a bounded recorded status. Two tests for the same personal-fever prompt have contradictory allowed intents: the security test requires `CLINICAL_CASE_CLARIFICATION`, while `test_simple_fever_question_routes_to_triage_and_asks_for_context` excludes it. The implementation now returns an explicit clinical-context clarification. The fifth failure revealed the Normens spelling gap and was subsequently corrected against the source joint monograph. Those historical assertions were not weakened or silently removed.

## Clinical relevance follow-up — 2026-11-06

The reviewed follow-up addresses four concrete subject/source boundaries. A general disease or unnamed treatment question must use relevant guidance instead of selecting a single drug monograph merely because its indications mention that disease. Oresol and other combinations without a combined-product source no longer expose component monograph doses or indications as though they described the product. A linked monograph with no clinical content may be replaced only by one exact ingredient and formulation-compatible source, otherwise the formatter abstains. Patient allergy queries resolve an explicit patient identifier or a unique exact full name before reading recorded allergies; the result is labelled patient-record information, not a prescription safety verdict.

Independent review reproduced and corrected these further defects before final verification:

Release modifiers and concentration denominators cannot be discarded. Ambiguous prose or presentation lists conservatively abstain. |
